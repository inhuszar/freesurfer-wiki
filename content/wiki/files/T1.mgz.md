# T1.mgz

> [!file] Glossary entry
> `T1.mgz` is the first intensity-normalised T1 volume, produced from [[nu.mgz]] by [[mri_normalize]] in autorecon1. It is a non-atlas-guided normalisation targeting white matter intensities to ~110. `T1.mgz` is the primary input to skull stripping ([[brainmask.mgz]]) and the second intensity normalisation step that creates [[brain.mgz]]. It differs from [[norm.mgz]], which is atlas-guided and used for segmentation.

## Location and Format

- **Canonical path:** `$SUBJECTS_DIR/<subj>/mri/T1.mgz`
- **Format:** [[mgz]] — MGH/MGZ binary; 256 × 256 × 256, 1 mm isotropic. Voxel type is typically `UCHAR` (intensity scaled to 0–255 with white matter near 110) or `FLOAT`.
- **Typical size / shape:** 256 × 256 × 256, ~16 MB gzipped.
- **Byte-accurate specification:** See [[mgz]].

## What It Contains

Each voxel holds a T1 intensity normalised so that cerebral white matter voxels have intensities near 110 (in the 0–255 `UCHAR` scale). Gray matter appears around 60–80, CSF near 0. This normalisation is not atlas-based — it is driven by control points estimated from the local white matter intensity histogram. The spatial grid and RAS frame are identical to [[orig.mgz]].

## How It Is Created

### Producing tool

[[mri_normalize]] — reads [[nu.mgz]], estimates the local white matter intensity bias field using a gradient-driven 3D normalization, and rescales voxel intensities so that WM ≈ 110. The output is `T1.mgz`.

```bash
# Default invocation (recon-all line 2204)
mri_normalize -g 1 nu.mgz T1.mgz

# With user-provided control points
mri_normalize -g 1 -f control.dat nu.mgz T1.mgz

# Base subject (saves ctrl_vol.mgz and bias_vol.mgz for longitudinal)
mri_normalize -g 1 -W ctrl_vol.mgz bias_vol.mgz nu.mgz T1.mgz
```

- [[nu.mgz]] — bias-field-corrected input.

### Siblings (co-produced outputs)

In the base-subject longitudinal stream, `ctrl_vol.mgz` and `bias_vol.mgz` are also written during the same invocation (recon-all line 2203).

## How It Is Used

### Direct downstream consumers

- [[mri_watershed]] — reads `T1.mgz` as input for skull stripping, producing [[brainmask.mgz]].
- [[mri_normalize]] (second invocation) — reads `T1.mgz` (via norm.mgz) with a brain mask to produce [[brain.mgz]].

### Downstream files derived from this one

- [[brainmask.mgz]] — skull-stripped brain mask derived from T1.
- [[brain.mgz]] — the final masked, normalised volume for surface placement.

## Alternative Names and Variants

### Aliases

In some older FreeSurfer workflows, `T1.mgz` was the primary reference volume used where [[orig.mgz]] is now used. In FS 8.x the two are distinct: `orig.mgz` is the raw conformed input; `T1.mgz` is the intensity-normalised version.

## Gotchas

> [!gotcha] T1.mgz is NOT the atlas-normalised volume
> Users sometimes confuse `T1.mgz` with [[norm.mgz]]. `T1.mgz` is produced during autorecon1 without atlas guidance and is used for skull stripping and surface placement. `norm.mgz` is produced during autorecon2 with atlas-based intensity normalisation and is used for segmentation. They differ in intensity calibration and should not be interchanged.
