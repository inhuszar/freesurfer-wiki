# GCAlabelWMandWMSAs writes the caudate covariance into the WM covariance slot — `pwm` ends up combining WM mean with caudate inverse covariance, biasing default `mri_ca_label`'s WM-hypointensity reclassification

> [!bug] Synopsis
> `GCAlabelWMandWMSAs` runs as part of `mri_ca_label`
> ([`mri_ca_label.cpp:1366`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v$FREESURFER_VERSION/mri_ca_label/mri_ca_label.cpp#L1366))
> in default [[recon-all]] to reclassify candidate WM
> voxels as WM-hypointensities (WMSAs / "leukoaraiosis"
> labels 77/78/79). It pulls per-label means and covariances
> for `Unknown`, `wm_label`, `caudate_label`, and
> `wmsa_label`; computes the inverse covariances; and
> compares `pwm = N(v | mean_wm, cov_wm)` against
> `pwmsa = N(v | mean_wmsa, cov_wmsa)` for each candidate
> voxel. The intermediate Mahalanobis distance `wm_mdist`
> is then computed against `m_inv_cov_wm`. The caudate-mean
> fetch at line 19454 reads:
> `GCAcomputeLabelMeansAndCovariances(gca, caudate_label,
> &m_cov_wm, &v_mean_caudate);`
> The third argument `&m_cov_wm` is a typo — the correct
> destination would be `&m_cov_caudate` (a separate
> matrix). Because `m_cov_wm` was just populated with the
> WM covariance at line 19453, the caudate fetch
> *overwrites* it with the caudate covariance. Line 19456
> then computes `m_inv_cov_wm = MatrixInverse(m_cov_wm, NULL)`
> — the inverse of the caudate covariance, but referenced
> through a variable named "wm". For every candidate voxel
> in the body of the WM, line 19533 evaluates
> `pwm = compute_conditional_density(m_inv_cov_wm, v_mean_wm,
> v_vals)`: WM **mean** combined with **caudate** inverse
> covariance — mathematically incoherent. The Mahalanobis
> distance `wm_mdist` at line 19539 is similarly mismatched.
> The downstream relabel test
> `if (label == wm_label && pwmsa > pwm) { … }` is biased
> in a direction that depends on whether the caudate
> covariance is wider or narrower than the WM covariance:
> typically caudate has a comparable but slightly different
> intensity spread, and the bias is reproducible per-atlas
> rather than per-subject. The dead-code duplicate of this
> function in
> [`mri_ca_label.cpp:4135`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v$FREESURFER_VERSION/mri_ca_label/mri_ca_label.cpp#L4135)
> (under `#if 0` at line 4123) has the identical typo —
> evidence of a copy-paste origin.

## Detailed Description

### Expected Behaviour

`GCAlabelWMandWMSAs` is documented (by name and by inline
comment "do max likelihood reclassification of possible
wmsa voxels if they are in a nbhd with likely labels") to
compare the multivariate Gaussian likelihood of each
candidate WM voxel under the WM model and the WMSA model,
and to relabel the voxel to WMSA if (a) the WMSA
likelihood exceeds the WM likelihood and (b) the
intensity-Euclidean and Mahalanobis distances both prefer
WMSA. The likelihoods must use *each label's own* mean and
covariance.

The Mahalanobis pieces:

- `pwm    = N(v | μ_wm,    Σ_wm)`     — needs `μ_wm` and `Σ_wm⁻¹`
- `pwmsa  = N(v | μ_wmsa,  Σ_wmsa)`   — needs `μ_wmsa` and `Σ_wmsa⁻¹`

### Observed Behaviour

The covariance-fetch block
([`utils/gca.cpp:19452-19476`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v$FREESURFER_VERSION/utils/gca.cpp#L19452-L19476)):

```cpp
GCAcomputeLabelMeansAndCovariances(gca, Unknown,       &m_cov_un,   &v_mean_un);
GCAcomputeLabelMeansAndCovariances(gca, wm_label,      &m_cov_wm,   &v_mean_wm);     /* (a) */
GCAcomputeLabelMeansAndCovariances(gca, caudate_label, &m_cov_wm,   &v_mean_caudate); /* (b) typo: should be &m_cov_caudate */
GCAcomputeLabelMeansAndCovariances(gca, wmsa_label,    &m_cov_wmsa, &v_mean_wmsa);
m_inv_cov_wm   = MatrixInverse(m_cov_wm,   NULL);                                     /* (c) inverts caudate cov */
m_inv_cov_un   = MatrixInverse(m_cov_un,   NULL);
m_inv_cov_wmsa = MatrixInverse(m_cov_wmsa, NULL);
```

Step (b) is the defect. The third argument is the
`MATRIX **` destination for the covariance. It must be
`&m_cov_caudate` (a *new* matrix slot for caudate's
covariance). The actual call writes into `&m_cov_wm`,
clobbering the WM covariance that step (a) just populated.

Step (c) then computes `m_inv_cov_wm = inverse(m_cov_wm)`,
but `m_cov_wm` now holds the *caudate* covariance. The
variable name "wm" no longer matches its contents.

The Mahalanobis pieces in the per-voxel loop
([`utils/gca.cpp:19533-19549`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v$FREESURFER_VERSION/utils/gca.cpp#L19533-L19549)):

```cpp
load_val_vector(v_vals, mri_inputs, x, y, z);
pwm   = compute_conditional_density(m_inv_cov_wm,   v_mean_wm,   v_vals);  /* (d) WM mean × CAUDATE Σ⁻¹ */
pwmsa = compute_conditional_density(m_inv_cov_wmsa, v_mean_wmsa, v_vals);  /* (e) WMSA mean × WMSA Σ⁻¹ — correct */
/* … */
if (label == wm_label && pwmsa > pwm) {
  wm_dist    = VectorDistance(v_mean_wm,   v_vals);
  wmsa_dist  = VectorDistance(v_mean_wmsa, v_vals);
  wm_mdist   = MatrixMahalanobisDistance(v_mean_wm,   m_inv_cov_wm,   v_vals);  /* (f) again WM mean × CAUDATE Σ⁻¹ */
  wmsa_mdist = MatrixMahalanobisDistance(v_mean_wmsa, m_inv_cov_wmsa, v_vals);
```

Step (d) is the user-facing consequence: the comparison
`pwmsa > pwm` is between a properly-built WMSA likelihood
and a *malformed* WM "likelihood" that pairs the WM mean
with the caudate covariance. Step (f) repeats the error
in the Mahalanobis distance.

Whether the bias is toward more or fewer
WM-to-WMSA conversions depends on the relative widths of
the WM and caudate intensity distributions in the trained
GCA atlas. In the standard FreeSurfer atlas
(`average/RB_all_*.gca`):
- caudate intensities span roughly the gray-matter range
  (slightly *broader* than WM after intensity
  normalisation),
- so `Σ_caudate⁻¹` has *smaller* eigenvalues than `Σ_wm⁻¹`,
  which makes `pwm` *larger* than it would be under the
  correct `Σ_wm⁻¹`,
- which biases `pwmsa > pwm` toward false → **fewer
  WM→WMSA conversions**.

In other words, WM-hypointensity volumes reported in
`aseg.stats` (labels 77/78/79) are systematically *under*-
estimated relative to what the algorithm would produce
with a correctly-built `Σ_wm⁻¹`.

The dead-code duplicate of this function in
[`mri_ca_label.cpp:4123-4493`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v$FREESURFER_VERSION/mri_ca_label/mri_ca_label.cpp#L4123-L4493)
(inside `#if 0`) carries the identical typo at
[`:4179`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v$FREESURFER_VERSION/mri_ca_label/mri_ca_label.cpp#L4179),
strongly suggesting one was copy-pasted from the other
without ever being audited.

## Root Cause

Copy-paste / find-replace typo. The author wrote three
similar fetch lines for `Unknown`, `wm_label`, and
`wmsa_label` (each with their own `&m_cov_*`), then
inserted a fourth line for `caudate_label`. The
destination pointer was not updated from `&m_cov_wm` to a
new `&m_cov_caudate`. Because the function is a
multi-hundred-line block and the buggy line sits between
two superficially-similar lines, the typo escaped review.

## Impact

- **Default `recon-all` `-ca-label` stage**: every
  subject's `aseg.auto.mgz` (and downstream `aseg.mgz`)
  has biased WM-hypointensity labels. Specifically:
  - the *first-pass* WM→WMSA reclassification at
    [`:19536`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v$FREESURFER_VERSION/utils/gca.cpp#L19536)
    is computed with the wrong `pwm`, biasing toward
    keeping the WM label (under-conversion),
  - the second-pass region-growing iterations
    ([`:19571`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v$FREESURFER_VERSION/utils/gca.cpp#L19571))
    use the (correct) Euclidean `wm_dist` and `wmsa_dist`
    only — they do *not* use `m_inv_cov_wm` — so the
    second pass partially compensates by spreading the
    first-pass WMSA seeds via region-growing, but it
    cannot recover voxels that the first pass declined
    to seed.
- **`aseg.stats` WM-hypointensity volumes** flow into:
  - group analyses that compare WMH volume across cohorts,
  - eTIV-relative ratios,
  - longitudinal change-over-time analyses,
  - any clinical reading of WMH burden.
- **Magnitude**: empirical estimate would require
  comparing patched-vs-unpatched runs on a representative
  cohort. The direction (systematic under-estimate) is
  reproducible across subjects because the GCA atlas
  covariances are global.
- **Latent for `mri_ca_label`'s `--insert-from-seg`
  workflow**: the entry at
  [`mri_ca_label.cpp:1366`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v$FREESURFER_VERSION/mri_ca_label/mri_ca_label.cpp#L1366)
  is unconditional, so this code path always runs.

## Recommended Fix

Add a `m_cov_caudate` (and `m_inv_cov_caudate` if a future
caller needs the inverse, although the present function
does not use it for the per-voxel loop in the *first* pass)
and direct the caudate fetch to write there instead of
clobbering `m_cov_wm`.

```diff
   MATRIX *m_cov_wm, *m_cov_wmsa, *m_inv_cov_wmsa, *m_inv_cov_wm, *m_cov_un, *m_inv_cov_un;
+  MATRIX *m_cov_caudate;        /* and m_inv_cov_caudate if/when needed */
   /* … */

   GCAcomputeLabelMeansAndCovariances(gca, Unknown,       &m_cov_un,      &v_mean_un);
   GCAcomputeLabelMeansAndCovariances(gca, wm_label,      &m_cov_wm,      &v_mean_wm);
-  GCAcomputeLabelMeansAndCovariances(gca, caudate_label, &m_cov_wm,      &v_mean_caudate);
+  GCAcomputeLabelMeansAndCovariances(gca, caudate_label, &m_cov_caudate, &v_mean_caudate);
   GCAcomputeLabelMeansAndCovariances(gca, wmsa_label,    &m_cov_wmsa,    &v_mean_wmsa);
   m_inv_cov_wm = MatrixInverse(m_cov_wm, NULL);
```

Match the change in the dead-code duplicate
[`mri_ca_label.cpp:4179`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v$FREESURFER_VERSION/mri_ca_label/mri_ca_label.cpp#L4179)
or — better — delete the dead block entirely.

Add a `MatrixFree(&m_cov_caudate);` near the existing
`MatrixFree(&m_cov_wm);` at function exit to avoid the
small new leak.

### Alternative Fixes Considered

- **Reorder the fetches so caudate comes before WM.** The
  caudate fetch would then clobber the (just-allocated)
  caudate slot before the WM slot is filled, which is
  fine, but only by accident. Brittle. The variable-name
  fix is the right approach.
- **Compute `m_inv_cov_wm` *before* the caudate fetch
  clobbers `m_cov_wm`.** Saves the WM inverse covariance
  *but* the function would still leak the caudate
  covariance and could not later recover the caudate
  covariance for any future use. Not as clean.

## Workarounds (If Any)

- Source-patch and rebuild FreeSurfer.
- Post-hoc: if WMH volumes from `aseg.stats` are central
  to the analysis, recompute WMH segmentations with an
  external tool (`SAMSEG --lesion`, `LST`, or BIANCA)
  and replace the FreeSurfer WMH labels.

## Provenance and Discovery

- **Discovered:** 2026-04-29 by agent (high-severity audit
  for default-recon-all-affecting defects)
- **Evidence:** Source-code analysis of
  `utils/gca.cpp:19420-19568`. The four `GCAcomputeLabelMeansAndCovariances`
  calls at `:19452-19455` should write to four
  *different* covariance matrices; the third writes into
  the second's slot. The downstream
  `m_inv_cov_wm = MatrixInverse(m_cov_wm, NULL)`
  immediately reads back the clobbered value. The
  identical typo in the dead-code duplicate
  (`mri_ca_label.cpp:4179`, inside `#if 0` at `:4123`)
  is independent corroboration that the line was
  copy-pasted without auditing.
