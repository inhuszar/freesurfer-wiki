# [severity-4] [bug-00200] `mri_cvs_register` / `surf2vol` — `-ksp_type preonly -pc_type jacobi` means the elastic FEM system is never solved: each step is one Jacobi scaling of the load, so the field stays zero outside elements that contain a boundary constraint

> [!bug] Synopsis
> [[mri_cvs_register]] runs its elastic stage ([[surf2vol]]) with
> `-ksp_rtol 1.0e-9 ... -pc_type jacobi -ksp_type preonly`. In PETSc,
> `preonly` applies the preconditioner **once** and performs no Krylov
> iteration, so with Jacobi every FEM step computes `u = D⁻¹ b` (the load
> divided by the diagonal of the system matrix) instead of solving
> `A u = b`; `-ksp_rtol` has no effect and the log reports
> `CONVERGED_ITS iterations 1` although the residual is large. The load is
> non-zero only at nodes of elements that hold an active boundary
> constraint, so all other nodes get exactly zero displacement in that step:
> the surface displacement is not propagated into the volume. In a controlled
> test (surface shifted rigidly by 3 mm) the white matter 10 voxels or more
> from the surface moved by 0.008 voxel instead of 3.000. The change from
> CG to `preonly` was made deliberately in 2011 and the script's parameters
> were tuned afterwards; whether the behaviour is the intended numerical scheme
> is not documented.

## Detailed Description

### What the call does

[`mri_cvs_register/mri_cvs_register:389-392`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v8.2.0/mri_cvs_register/mri_cvs_register#L389-L392)
sets the elastic options once for all variants of the call:

```csh
  set elasticoptions = (-lin_res 20 -ksp_rtol 1.0e-9 -penalty_weight 1000 -fem_steps 17 -elt_vol_range 5.0,10.0 \
  #set elasticoptions = (-lin_res 20 -ksp_rtol 1.0e-9 -penalty_weight 500 -fem_steps 17 -elt_vol_range 5.0,10.0 \
                       -topology_old -compress -pc_type jacobi -ksp_type preonly \
                       -ksp_view -ksp_converged_reason -poisson .3 -young 1000)
```

`surf2vol` assembles, for each of the `-fem_steps` (17 here, plus one extra
step: 18 solves, because `-fem_end_step` defaults to -1,
[`fem_elastic/fsurf2vol.cpp:1240`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v8.2.0/fem_elastic/fsurf2vol.cpp#L1240)),
a linear-elasticity stiffness matrix `K` on a tetrahedral mesh of the
template box (`-young`, `-poisson`) and enforces the surface correspondences
as **penalty constraints** ("MFC": multi-freedom constraints, one 3-D
displacement target per boundary point). Each surface vertex pair gives a
displacement `δ = (moving − fixed) / step` at the fixed-surface point
([`fem_elastic/fsurf2vol.cpp:819-828`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v8.2.0/fem_elastic/fsurf2vol.cpp#L819-L828)),
and eight box corners are pinned to zero
([`:805-817`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v8.2.0/fem_elastic/fsurf2vol.cpp#L805-L817)).
Per element at most **one** constraint is active (the one whose `δ` is closest to
the mean of the constraints in that element,
[`fem_elastic/solver.h:611-638`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v8.2.0/fem_elastic/solver.h#L611-L638)).
For each active constraint the system matrix receives `w·NᵀN` on the nodes of
its element (`w` = `-penalty_weight`) and the right-hand side receives
`w·Nᵀδ` ([`add_elt_mfc_lhs`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v8.2.0/fem_elastic/solver.h#L749-L820),
[`add_elt_mfc_rhs`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v8.2.0/fem_elastic/solver.h#L845-L908),
driven by [`setup_load_mfc`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v8.2.0/fem_elastic/solver.h#L823-L842)).
The natural (pinned-corner) conditions contribute `K·0 = 0` to the load
([`fem_elastic/solver.h:1062-1067`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v8.2.0/fem_elastic/solver.h#L1062-L1067),
[`:1115-1121`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v8.2.0/fem_elastic/solver.h#L1115-L1121)).
So the load vector `b` is non-zero **only at the nodes of elements that carry an
active constraint**.

The system is then solved by PETSc
([`fem_elastic/solver.h:364-389`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v8.2.0/fem_elastic/solver.h#L364-L389)):
the code sets a relative tolerance of 1e-9 and calls `KSPSetFromOptions`,
so the command line chooses the method.

### What `preonly` + `jacobi` computes

`KSPPREONLY` skips the Krylov iteration and applies the preconditioner exactly
once to the right-hand side. With `-pc_type jacobi` that is
`u_i = b_i / A_ii` for every degree of freedom, `A` being the assembled
matrix (stiffness plus penalty terms). Consequences that follow directly from
the assembly above:

1. For every node that does not belong to an element with an active constraint,
   `b_i = 0` and therefore `u_i = 0` **exactly**, no matter how large the
   neighbouring displacements are: there is no coupling through the off-diagonal
   entries of `K`, i.e. no elastic propagation.
2. For nodes of constraint elements `u_i = w·Σ N_i δ / A_ii` — only the diagonal
   enters, so `-young` and `-poisson` act only through the diagonal of `K`,
   and the displacement at the constraint point is not `δ`.
3. `-ksp_rtol 1.0e-9` is irrelevant. PETSc reports
   `Linear solve converged due to CONVERGED_ITS iterations 1` and `Iterations 1`
   for `preonly` regardless of the quality of `u`. The program computes the
   residual `‖Ku − b‖` and prints it as `Absolute-Norm of error`, but never
   tests it, and prints the convergence reason without acting on it
   ([`fem_elastic/solver.h:386-417`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v8.2.0/fem_elastic/solver.h#L386-L417)).

The 18 steps re-run this scaling on the updated constraint positions, so the
constraint points approach their targets over the steps while the
interior receives displacement only through the elements that *at some step*
contain an active constraint.

For comparison, the iterative solver that the topology repair uses is
explicit CG + Jacobi on `direct_`-prefixed options
([`fem_elastic/solver.h:1252-1272`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v8.2.0/fem_elastic/solver.h#L1252-L1272)),
so the same matrix class is solved properly there, and the main solver had
(commented-out) code that selected Jacobi from inside the program
([`fem_elastic/solver.h:369-370`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v8.2.0/fem_elastic/solver.h#L369-L370)).
The matrix is flagged symmetric
([`fem_elastic/solver.h:662-665`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v8.2.0/fem_elastic/solver.h#L662-L665))
and CG converged in all tests below.

### Expected Behaviour

A finite-element elasticity registration should return the displacement field
that satisfies the constraints and the equilibrium equations; i.e. the system
should be solved to the requested tolerance (`-ksp_rtol 1.0e-9` is passed
explicitly for that purpose), so that surface displacements are transmitted
into the white matter and subcortical structures through the elastic coupling.

### Observed Behaviour

## Root Cause

The elastic stage depends on an iterative linear solve, but the caller selects
a method that performs none. PETSc's `preonly` KSP type applies the
preconditioner once and stops; combined with the Jacobi (diagonal)
preconditioner this reduces each FEM step to `u = diag(A)⁻¹ b`. Because `b` is
non-zero only on the nodes of constraint-carrying elements, the solution is
zero everywhere else. `TSolver::solve` accepts any KSP/PC from the command line
and neither checks the residual it prints nor warns about a non-iterative
method, so the mis-configuration is silent.

### History

`git log -S` / `git show` on the script: from commit `c5f9b59510` (2011-05-04, where
the options moved from a separate settings file into the script) until
`3916ce1fbc` they read
`-pc_type none -ksp_type preonly -pc_type jacobi -ksp_type cg` (PETSc keeps the
last occurrence, so effectively CG + Jacobi, verified above; the 2010 settings file
`mri_cvs_register.settings.txt`, commit `d65ed2d83d`, has the same string and an
`-openmp_ksp_type cg` variant for the MPI/OpenMP case). Commit `3916ce1fbc`
(2011-10-20, Lilla Zollei, message "changed the young modulus default and pc and
ksp types") replaced it, in the same change that enabled
`-elt_vol_range 5.0,10.0`, `-poisson .3` and `-young 1000`:

```diff
- set elasticoptions = (-lin_res 20 -ksp_rtol 1.0e-9 -penalty_weight 1000 -fem_steps 17 \ #-elt_vol_range 5.0,10.0 \
-                       -log_summary -topology_old -compress -pc_type none -ksp_type preonly \
-                       -pc_type jacobi -ksp_type cg -ksp_view -ksp_converged_reason )
+ set elasticoptions = (-lin_res 20 -ksp_rtol 1.0e-9 -penalty_weight 1000 -fem_steps 17 -elt_vol_range 5.0,10.0 \
+                       -log_summary -topology_old -compress -pc_type jacobi -ksp_type preonly \
+                       -ksp_view -ksp_converged_reason -poisson .3 -young 1000)
```

The `-pc_type jacobi -ksp_type preonly` pair has been unchanged since (later
commits such as `557aadb10c` and `1e22ef2da4` re-wrapped the lines and changed
other options). The reason for the switch is not recorded.

## Impact

- **Default `mri_cvs_register` path:** the elastic stage, whose purpose is to
  carry the surface-based correspondence into the volume, only displaces the
  neighbourhood of the cortical surfaces; the deep white matter and subcortical
  structures keep the affine estimate (79 % of deep-WM voxels had no non-affine
  displacement in the synthetic test, against 12 % with CG). The later
  volumetric stages ([[mri_nl_align]] on intensity and aseg, combined with the
  elastic warp by [[mri_concatenate_gcam]],
  [`mri_cvs_register/mri_cvs_register:603-606`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v8.2.0/mri_cvs_register/mri_cvs_register#L603-L606))
  work on the result and may compensate in part; the size of what remains in
  the final CVS warp was not measured.
- **Parameters interact with the behaviour:** the script's comment block
  ([`mri_cvs_register/mri_cvs_register:374-387`](https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/v8.2.0/mri_cvs_register/mri_cvs_register#L374-L387):
  "it is not really the individual values but the ratio of penalty_weight and
  young that counts", "steps = 1: the output is very fragmented; the diffusion
  process only begins") documents empirically tuned `penalty_weight`, `young` and
  step count; with a real solve those would behave differently, so changing the
  solver requires re-tuning (see [[00207]] for the missing element-volume factor,
  which changes the per-node diagonal and hence the preonly step).
- **Silent:** the output is a valid-looking warp; the only indications are the
  `Iterations 1` line and a residual of order 1e5 in the log, which the
  script never checks.
- **Severity 4, not 5:** the defect is always on in [[mri_cvs_register]] and
  changes the default registration, but [[mri_cvs_register]] is not one of the
  severity-5 pipelines (it is reached from [[trac-all]] only for the CVS
  inter-subject space and from [[post-recon-all]] only with `--cvs`); the
  design intent is uncertain (the option string was changed on purpose and all
  tuning followed); and the later volumetric steps refine the result.
- **Existing CVS results:** every CVS warp produced since the 2011-10-20 change
  of the script shares this behaviour; a fix would change them.

## Recommended Fix

There is no unambiguous code defect to patch: the decision between "document
the scheme as a Jacobi diffusion" and "solve the system" belongs to the
developers (see the gap below). The candidate that restores the pre-2011-10
behaviour and satisfies the explicit `-ksp_rtol 1.0e-9`:

```diff
-                       -topology_old -compress -pc_type jacobi -ksp_type preonly \
+                       -topology_old -compress -pc_type jacobi -ksp_type cg \
```

(`mri_cvs_register/mri_cvs_register:391`). `A` is flagged symmetric
(`MatSetOption(..., MAT_SYMMETRIC)`) and CG converged in all tests (1176–2386 iterations per
solve at 1e-6–1e-9; 1.2 min vs 2.1 min for the 3-step synthetic test).
`penalty_weight`, `young`, `poisson` and `-fem_steps` should be re-tuned and the
resulting warps compared with the current CVS output (and with
`mri_nl_align` results) before adopting it.

Independent hardening in `TSolver::solve` (does not change CVS results with
`preonly`):

```diff
   // solve
   ierr = KSPSolve(ksp, m_load, m_delta);
   CHKERRQ(ierr);

   KSPConvergedReason reason;
   ierr = KSPGetConvergedReason(ksp, &reason);
   CHKERRQ(ierr);
   std::cout << " convergence reason = " << reason << std::endl;
+  if ( reason < 0 )
+    std::cerr << " WARNING: linear solve diverged (reason " << reason << ")\n";
```

plus a warning when the KSP type is `preonly` or the final residual
`‖Ku − b‖ / ‖b‖` exceeds the requested tolerance. A regression test is the
rigid-shift experiment above: a 3 mm translation of the surface must give a
displacement of 3 mm (to a tolerance of a few hundredths) in white matter far
from the surface.

### Alternative Fixes Considered

- **Keep `preonly`/Jacobi as an intended explicit relaxation scheme (untested alternatives):** document
  it in the script and in [[surf2vol]] and drop the misleading `-ksp_rtol`; or
  implement a fixed number of damped Jacobi sweeps (`-ksp_type richardson
  -pc_type jacobi -ksp_max_it N`) so that propagation depth is controlled
  and reported.
- **Preconditioner with off-diagonal coupling** (ICC, algebraic multigrid) with CG (untested).
  Faster per iteration count but the default PETSc preconditioner chain segfaulted in the
  solve in these tests (see the [[surf2vol]] page), so any change must be tested on
  this PETSc 2.3.3 build.

## Workarounds (If Any)

For a registration that propagates the surface displacement into the volume,
call `surf2vol` directly with `-ksp_type cg -pc_type jacobi` (verified to
converge on `cvs_avg35`), expecting different results from `mri_cvs_register`.
The script has no option to change the solver; its `elasticoptions` must be
edited. Re-tuning of `-penalty_weight`/`-young` is advisable.

## Confidence and Gaps

> [!gap] Was `preonly` + `jacobi` a deliberate numerical scheme?
> The mechanism (one diagonal scaling per step, zero field outside constraint
> elements) is established by the code and by experiment. Whether the
> developers intended a diffusion-like scheme, chose `preonly` for speed (CG needed 1100–2400 iterations per solve in
> these tests), or as a workaround for the PETSc default solver (which segfaults in the
> solve in these tests) is not recorded: commit `3916ce1fbc` changed from CG to
> `preonly` explicitly and enabled `-young`/`-poisson` in the same change. The
> script comment "steps = 1: the output is very fragmented; the diffusion
> process only begins" is consistent with tuning around this behaviour.
> Developer confirmation is needed before this is treated as a defect rather
> than a design limitation.

> [!gap] Effect on the final CVS warp
> The deep-white-matter deficit was measured on the elastic output of
> controlled and synthetic deformations, not on the final CVS warp after
> [[mri_nl_align]] and [[mri_concatenate_gcam]], nor on real subject pairs.
